# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.  
It is a system used by companies to manage customer data, interactions, sales, and services in one place.

A CRM helps businesses:
- Store customer information
- Track communication
- Manage sales processes
- Improve customer relationships
- Increase productivity

---

## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce

Salesforce is one of the most popular CRM platforms in the world.

Companies use Salesforce because it:
- Stores customer data securely
- Helps sales teams track opportunities
- Automates business processes
- Improves customer support
- Provides reports and dashboards
- Works on cloud technology
- Increases business efficiency

---

## 3. Salesforce Objects Explanation

### Account
An Account represents a company or organization.

Example:
- TCS
- Infosys
- Amazon

Accounts store company-related information such as:
- Company name
- Industry
- Phone number
- Website

---

### Contact
A Contact represents a person associated with an Account.

Example:
- Employee name
- Customer name
- Manager details

Contacts contain:
- First Name
- Last Name
- Email
- Phone Number

---

### Opportunity
An Opportunity represents a potential sales deal.

It helps companies track:
- Deal amount
- Sales stage
- Closing date
- Probability of winning the deal

Example:
A company planning to purchase software services from another company.

---

## 4. Real-World Mapping

| Real World | Salesforce Object |
|------------|------------------|
| Company | Account |
| Person working in company | Contact |
| Business deal or sale | Opportunity |

### Example:
- Company: Amazon → Account
- Employee: Rahul Sharma → Contact
- Software Purchase Deal → Opportunity
